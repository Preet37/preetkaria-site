Adaptive Perception Pipelines: Facial Recognition on Edge Hardware

Sole author · Baskin School of Engineering, UCSC

Comparative analysis of Haar cascades against CNN embeddings on edge hardware. Haar runs at 12 ms per frame against the CNN pipeline's 180 ms, but accuracy splits hard by conditions: 92/99 in ideal lighting, 68/98 under harsh shadows, 45/95 on profile views. The conclusion is that latency buys you nothing once the environment stops being controlled.

Kinematic & Energetic Trade-offs of Gastropod Loping Gaits

Research · Baskin School of Engineering

A five-link planar crawler trained with PPO in MuJoCo. Learned lope holds CoT ≈ 1.2–1.3 flat against friction; dragging scales as μ. They cross at μ* ≈ 1.35.
